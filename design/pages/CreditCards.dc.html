Questions, answered

Will checking my eligibility affect my credit score?

No. The eligibility checker uses a soft search, which only you can see. A full credit check happens only if you go on to apply.

How long does it take to get my card?

Most applications get a decision in minutes. Your card arrives within 3 to 5 working days, and you can add it to your mobile wallet as soon as you're approved.

Can I transfer a balance from another bank?

Yes. With SecurBank Clear you can transfer balances from other cards within 60 days of opening your account. A 3% fee applies to each transfer.

What happens if I can't make a repayment?

Talk to us as early as you can. Our support team can look at your options with you, including a temporary payment plan.
